feat: the noise-aware statement wired end to end, so P = A*p + e1 is provable

C1's finding was that the stealth public key is an MLWE sample, not an exact linear image,
and that SMILE's exact relation therefore rejects a legitimately produced key. This is the
wiring that fixes it:

  matrix   A_noise = [A | I_k]      13 + 4 = 17 columns
  witness  (p, e1)                  one short vector of length 17
  public   A*p + e1 = P             the noisy key the stealth derivation produces

A full prove and verify over that statement now completes at ell = 17, accepted against the
right message and rejected against a different one. The witness is checked short before
proving, because the proof's bounds apply to the whole vector and an oversized noise term
would fail for the wrong reason.

What this does NOT establish: soundness of the noise-aware statement. The extraction
argument is C1's, it is not written, and docs/PAPER.md lists it under the limitations rather
than implying the wiring makes it proven. What is established is that the statement shape
works with the existing proof at the required width.

Two prerequisites made this a single pass where three earlier attempts failed: ell = 17
being constructible at all, and Parameters carrying its SmileParameters.

One slow test, 133 s, because it produces a real 17-column proof.
