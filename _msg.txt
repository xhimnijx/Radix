feat: base-4 addition with an explicit carry, which is what balance needs

Reference [25] p. 4 gives the identity SMILE uses for balance and documents/CONFIDENTIAL-TRANSACTIONS.md section 5 stopped at. Implemented at r = 4:

    a + b + f*(X - 4) = c

over polynomials, not merely over Z_q, because the evaluation at X = 4 is what turns a digit polynomial back into its integer and a relation that only held at that point would say nothing.

Two findings, both now tested.

The carry for adding TWO amounts is binary (at most 1, since a digit sum is at most 6). That is why a two-term addition needs a bit constraint rather than the relaxed proof -- and it means the relaxed proof is only needed once many inputs are summed, which is exactly the multi-input case SMILE has.

The relation must be checked coefficient by coefficient. There is a test that perturbs the carry by (X - 4), which vanishes at X = 4 and leaves the evaluated identity intact: a check that only evaluated would accept it, and the coefficient-wise check rejects it.

Also fixed a tautology I had written into that very test -- `assert ... is not True or True`, which is always true and asserted nothing. Replaced with the real assertion it was meant to be.

7 tests. The carry is computed by long addition in base 4 so a caller cannot manufacture a relation that does not exist, and an inconsistent addition raises rather than returning a carry.
