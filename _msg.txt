feat: checkpointed sync, which completes the network hardening item

A node refreshes a snapshot of its own state every CHECKPOINT_INTERVAL blocks, and
sync_from replays from it instead of from genesis -- but only when the peer's chain hashes
to the same block at that height.

That agreement check is the whole safety argument. A snapshot is a statement about a
PARTICULAR chain; applying it to a different one would adopt a state that never existed.
When the hashes disagree, or the restore raises, the code falls back to a full rebuild and
logs why rather than proceeding on a bad snapshot.

The measured win is modest and was measured before this was built: 1.1x at 200 empty
blocks, break-even around 73 skipped transactions, because restoring costs a fixed 197 ms
that is mostly regenerating the decoy pool. The tests check correctness and the fallback
rather than speed, and the module docstring says so.

replay_from now takes verify_transactions and defaults to True, matching rebuild: a chain
from a peer is verified whatever route it arrives by.

Network hardening is complete: authenticated transport, multi-hop discovery, slow-start
bootstrap, and checkpoints in sync.

5 checkpoint-sync tests, 25 across that and the p2p suite.
