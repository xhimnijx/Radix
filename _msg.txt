feat: the range proof's alpha-slot terms, as functions

Steps 3 and 4 of docs/ALPHA-SLOTS.md, factored out so they can be tested before the proof's
arity changes. Nothing here touches the wire format.

  psi_range   = sum eta_b * a_b * (1 - 2b)
  omega_range = sum eta_b * a_b^2

The number of terms is checked rather than assumed: a silent length mismatch would drop a
constraint, which is the kind of failure that looks like a passing proof.

DELIBERATELY NOT INCLUDED: the cancellation condition. I wrote a `combined` helper computing
`psi + c*omega` and a test that it vanishes for binary bits, and both were wrong -- the
verifier's check is `omega - c*psi`, since the commitment's square expands to
c^2 v(v-1) + c a(2v-1) + a^2 and with b binary the c^2 term dies leaving a^2 - c*a(1-2v).
Rather than fix it in the remaining budget I removed both, with a note explaining why: the
equation belongs in the verifier once, not in a helper and the verifier twice. Writing it in
two places is exactly the class of bug this project has paid for four times.

Two of my own test errors, both from assumptions about the ring: the first compared vectors
of fours expecting coefficient-wise scaling when ring.mul is a convolution, and the second
compared whole polynomials when the representation carries trailing zeros. The property is
now tested by its centred value, which is what (1 - 2b) actually claims.

6 tests: the b = 0 and b = 1 cases give +a and -a, omega squares, the shape mismatch is
refused, the transcript draws in order, and a shorter draw is a prefix of a longer one --
which is what makes the challenge stream usable.
