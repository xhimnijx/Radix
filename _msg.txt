feat: a spend can carry a range witness, checked before anything else

The data-model step before the proof change, and deliberately the safe half of it. A spend
can now carry the base-4 digits and the packed bits for every amount, and the node checks
their internal consistency on decode -- before the rest of the envelope -- so a malformed
witness is rejected for the right reason rather than deep inside proof verification.

What this establishes: the encoding, the 32-digit packing, the 2-bits-per-digit relation
and the slot-binaryness check all run on real traffic. What it does not: the witness is
checked IN THE CLEAR, so it proves nothing to a verifier who does not trust the prover. The
limitation is named in docs/PAPER.md section 8 rather than implied by the field's presence.

Seven tests: the witness round-trips and its bits reconstruct its digits; a consistent one
is accepted; an absent one is accepted, since a visible-amount spend is the current shape;
and four refusals -- bits disagreeing with digits, a non-binary bit, a digit above 3, and a
malformed structure with a clear error.

The end-to-end and chain suites still pass, so a normal spend is unaffected: the witness is
optional and absent by default.

Next: the alpha slots. That is the change that turns this witness from a claim into a
proof, and it touches the commitment arity, the transcript, the wire format and the
Gaussian tables together -- which is why it is not being started at the end of a round.
